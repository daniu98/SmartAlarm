# SmartAlarm Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 August 2026_

**SmartAlarm collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.**

Everything the app knows about you lives in a database on your iPhone and is deleted when you
delete the app. The developer cannot see any of it, because it is never sent anywhere.

## What stays on your device

| | |
|---|---|
| Home and work addresses, and their coordinates | Stored locally, entered by you |
| Alarm times, leave-by times and your settings | Stored locally |
| Check history — every traffic check and its result | Stored locally |
| Travel time samples used to learn your commute | Stored locally |
| Your calendar events and reminders, if you enable that | **Read, never stored** — used in memory to work out one arrival time, then discarded |

## What leaves your device, and where it goes

Three things, all to Apple, none to the developer:

1. **Route lookups.** Your home and destination coordinates are sent to Apple Maps to estimate
   driving time. Governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
2. **Address lookups.** When you type an address in Setup, it is sent to Apple Maps to convert
   it to coordinates. Same terms.
3. **Weather forecasts** (Pro only). Your departure coordinates are sent to Apple WeatherKit.
   Same terms. If you don't buy Pro, this request is never made.

The developer operates no servers and receives none of this data.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Alarms** — required. SmartAlarm uses a system alarm so it rings even when the app is closed
  and your phone is silenced.
- **Calendar** — optional, Pro. Read-only. Used to find your first commitment of the morning so
  the alarm can move *earlier*. Events are never stored or transmitted.
- **Reminders** — optional, Pro. Read-only. Only the titles of high-priority reminders due that
  morning, used to decide whether to add extra buffer.

The app does **not** request location access. It never tracks where you are; it only routes
between the two addresses you typed in.

## Purchases

SmartAlarm Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your
payment details. Whether you own Pro is checked against the App Store on your device.

## Children

SmartAlarm is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Since the
app collects nothing, any change would be to clarify rather than to expand what is gathered.
