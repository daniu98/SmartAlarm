Privacy Policy

SmartyAlarm collects nothing.

HomeSupportPrivacy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

SmartyAlarm collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

Everything the app knows about you lives in a database on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete the app. The developer cannot see any of it, because it is never sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

Home and work addresses, and their coordinatesStored locally, entered by you
Alarm times, leave-by times and your settingsStored locally
Check history — every traffic check and its resultStored locally
Travel time samples used to learn your commuteStored locally
Your calendar events and reminders, if you enable thatRead, never stored — used in memory to work out one arrival time, then discarded

What leaves your device, and where it goes

Three things, all to Apple, none to the developer:

  1. Route lookups. Your home and destination coordinates are sent to Apple Maps to estimate driving time, under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  2. Address lookups. When you type an address in Setup it is sent to Apple Maps to convert it to coordinates. Same terms.
  3. Weather forecasts (Pro only). Your departure coordinates are sent to Apple WeatherKit. Same terms. Without Pro this request is never made.

The developer operates no servers and receives none of this data.

Permissions

AlarmsRequired. Used so the alarm rings even when the app is closed and the phone is silenced.
CalendarOptional, Pro. Read-only. Used to find your first commitment so the alarm can move earlier. Never stored or transmitted.
RemindersOptional, Pro. Read-only. Only titles of high-priority reminders due that morning.
LocationNever requested. The app does not track where you are.

Purchases

SmartyAlarm Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details. Whether you own Pro is checked against the App Store on your device.

Children

SmartyAlarm is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

Any update to this policy will be posted here with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, a change would be to clarify rather than to expand what is gathered.
